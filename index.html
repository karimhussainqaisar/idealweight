About Ideal Body Weight

The Ideal Weight Calculator computes ideal body weight (IBW) ranges based on height, gender, and age. These formulas were originally developed for medical dosage calculations but are now used as general health guidelines. Note that IBW is an estimate and does not account for body composition, muscle mass, or individual health conditions. Always consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.
